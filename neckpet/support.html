FAQ (English)

Why doesn't the floating window appear in the simulator?

Picture in Picture may behave differently in the iOS Simulator. Check it on a physical iPhone.

Posture tracking isn't updating

In your iPhone's Settings, make sure Motion & Fitness is enabled for NeckPet. If you use headphone tracking, check that compatible headphones are connected.

What's the circle with the NeckPet icon next to the Dynamic Island?

On iOS 26 and later, that's iOS's own indicator for NeckPet's background session. It keeps posture tracking going while you use other apps. iOS draws it and decides where it sits; NeckPet can't hide or restyle it. Your pet appears in its own circle with a green (good posture) or yellow (look up) ring. Tap End session in NeckPet to stop both.

Tracking stopped while I was in another app

iOS can end background time to save power, and tapping Stop on iOS's indicator ends it too. Your session stays on: the Dynamic Island shows “Open NeckPet for a new reading”, tracking continues while NeckPet is open, and one tap on “Resume background tracking” turns background tracking back on. Home → Connection log lists recent events, such as headphones pausing motion, with their times.

I don't hear the reminder ding

In NeckPet, the ding plays only in headphones. In the background, the reminder notification uses the same sound, so Silent mode, Focus and Settings → Notifications → NeckPet → Sounds all apply. Banners appear at most once a minute; the in-app buzz and ding can repeat every 15 seconds.

Does NeckPet drain my battery?

Continuous motion tracking uses some battery, a little more when it runs in the background. End the session when you don't need it.

Why am I asked to sign in when restoring purchases?

Apple may ask you to sign in to verify your purchases.

常见问题（中文）

为什么模拟器里 PiP 不稳定？

iOS Simulator 对 PiP 支持有限，真实设备上的行为更准确。建议在真机测试。

为什么姿态检测没有变化？

请在系统设置中确认 NeckPet 已开启“运动与健身”权限；耳机模式下请确认连接了兼容耳机。

灵动岛旁边带 NeckPet 图标的圆圈是什么？

在 iOS 26 及以上，这是 iOS 为 NeckPet 后台陪伴显示的系统指示器，它让你在使用其他 App 时也能继续检测姿势。它由 iOS 绘制并决定位置，NeckPet 无法隐藏或修改。你的宠物显示在自己的圆圈里：绿圈表示姿势良好，黄圈提醒你抬头。在 NeckPet 中点「停止陪伴」可同时关闭两者。

切到其他 App 后检测停了

iOS 可能为了省电收回后台时间，点系统指示器上的停止按钮也会结束后台陪伴。陪伴本身不会被关闭：灵动岛会显示「打开 NeckPet 更新姿势」，打开 NeckPet 时照常检测，点一下「继续后台陪伴」即可恢复后台检测。陪伴页的「连接记录」会列出最近的事件（例如耳机暂停发送动作数据）和时间。

听不到提醒的「叮」声

在 App 内，「叮」只在耳机里播放。在后台时，提醒通知使用同一个声音，因此会受静音模式、专注模式以及「设置 → 通知 → NeckPet → 声音」影响。通知横幅每分钟最多一条；App 内的震动和「叮」最快每 15 秒一次。

NeckPet 耗电吗？

持续检测姿势会消耗一些电量，后台运行时会稍多一点。不需要时请停止陪伴。

恢复购买为什么会弹 Apple ID 密码？

这是 Apple 恢复购买流程的常见行为，属于系统层面的正常提示。
