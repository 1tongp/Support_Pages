NeckPet Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: November 22, 2026

English

NeckPet works on your device. We don't run servers, we don't collect your data, and you don't need an account.

1. Motion readings

With your permission (Motion & Fitness), NeckPet reads motion from your iPhone or compatible headphones such as AirPods. They are used only to work out whether you're looking down and to set your pet's mood. Readings are processed in real time on your device. They are not stored as a history and never leave your device.

2. Background sessions

On iOS 26 and later, after you tap Start, NeckPet can keep checking your posture while you use other apps. iOS shows its own progress indicator and a Stop control. The session ends when you stop it, close NeckPet, or iOS ends it.

3. Game progress and settings

Coins, pets, accessories, food, bond points, streaks, language and reminder settings are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, some of this progress syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). We can't see it. You can turn this off for NeckPet in iCloud settings.

4. Notifications, Dynamic Island and Lock Screen

If you allow notifications, NeckPet sends local posture reminders from your device. Your pet and posture status may appear on the Dynamic Island and the Lock Screen (Live Activities) or in a floating window (Picture in Picture). People nearby may be able to see these. You can turn them off in Settings.

5. Connection log

NeckPet keeps a short on-device list of recent tracking events, such as “headphones stopped sending motion”, to help explain dropouts. It holds only the event type and time, stays on your device, and you can clear it in the app.

6. In-app purchases

Some pets can be bought. Apple handles payment through the App Store; we don't receive your payment details.

7. Sharing, tracking and analytics

NeckPet doesn't sell or share personal data, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

8. Deleting your data

Deleting NeckPet removes its data from your device. To remove synced progress too, delete NeckPet's data in your iCloud storage settings.

9. Children

NeckPet doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. For questions, contact: daisy0822@hotmail.com

中文

NeckPet 的功能都在你的设备上完成。我们没有服务器，不收集你的数据，也不需要注册账号。

1. 运动数据

在你允许“运动与健身”权限后，NeckPet 会读取 iPhone 或兼容耳机（如 AirPods）的运动数据，只用来判断你是否在低头，并据此改变宠物的心情。数据在设备上实时处理，不保存历史记录，也不会离开你的设备。

2. 后台陪伴

在 iOS 26 及以上，点击“开始”后，NeckPet 可以在你使用其他 App 时继续检测姿势。iOS 会显示系统的进度提示和停止按钮。你停止陪伴、关闭 NeckPet 或 iOS 结束任务时，后台检测都会停止。

3. 游戏进度与设置

金币、宠物、配饰、食物、亲密度、连胜、语言和提醒设置都保存在你的设备上。如果你登录了 iCloud，部分进度会通过你自己的 iCloud 账户（iCloud 键值存储）在你的设备间同步，我们无法查看。你可以在 iCloud 设置中为 NeckPet 关闭同步。

4. 通知、灵动岛与锁屏

如果你允许通知，NeckPet 会从本机发送姿势提醒。宠物和姿势状态可能显示在灵动岛、锁屏（实时活动）或悬浮窗（画中画）中，身边的人可能看得到。你可以在系统设置中关闭。

5. 连接记录

为了帮你排查“掉线”，NeckPet 会在本机保存最近几条检测事件（例如“耳机暂停发送动作数据”），只包含事件类型和时间，不会上传，可以在 App 中清空。

6. 应用内购买

部分宠物可以购买。付款由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不会收到你的支付信息。

7. 共享、追踪与分析

NeckPet 不出售或共享个人数据，不跨 App 或网站追踪你，也不包含广告或第三方分析工具。

8. 删除数据

删除 NeckPet 即可清除设备上的数据。如需同时删除已同步的进度，请在 iCloud 储存空间设置中删除 NeckPet 的数据。

9. 儿童

NeckPet 不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

10. 变更与联系方式

本政策如有更新，我们会修改本页面及生效日期。如有疑问，请联系： daisy0822@hotmail.com
