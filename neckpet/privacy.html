NeckPet Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: March 10, 2026

English

NeckPet respects your privacy. This app is designed to work with local on-device processing.

1. Data We Use

  • Motion and posture signals from device motion and/or compatible headphones.
  • Local gameplay data such as coins, inventory, streak progress, and settings.

2. How Data Is Used

  • Motion data is used in real time to determine pet mood and posture status.
  • Gameplay data is used to provide progression, rewards, and cosmetic unlocks.

3. Data Sharing

NeckPet does not sell your personal data. No account registration is required.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through App Store billing.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact: daisy0822@hotmail.com

中文

NeckPet 尊重你的隐私。本应用以本地设备处理为核心，不依赖账号系统。

1. 我们会使用的数据

  • 设备运动数据与兼容耳机姿态数据（用于姿势识别）。
  • 本地游戏数据，例如金币、背包、连胜进度、语言设置等。

2. 数据用途

  • 实时判断姿势状态并更新宠物情绪（开心/难过）。
  • 用于本地成长、奖励发放与外观解锁。

3. 数据共享

NeckPet 不会出售你的个人信息，也不要求注册账号。

4. 应用内购买

应用内购买由 Apple App Store 支付系统处理。

5. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：daisy0822@hotmail.com
